### Selected Projects

**[Medicare Part D Spending: Price vs. Volume](https://github.com/krithihari2/drug-spending-price-vs-volume)**
Analyzed CMS data on 3,625 drugs to explain a $94.6B increase in Medicare Part D spending from 2020 to 2024. Growth came from greater use of existing drugs (67%) and new drugs (33%), not from price increases on the same drugs. Fifteen drugs accounted for 70% of the increase.
SQL, Python, pandas

**[Forecasting FTSE 100 Returns](FTSE-LINK)**
Tested whether U.S. equities, UK bond yields and GBP/USD help forecast FTSE 100 returns. Found a strong same-month link to U.S. markets and an inverse relationship between the pound and FTSE returns.
EViews, VAR, Granger causality, Excel
